# Positioning & Principles

The logical case for why we're building this, what makes it different, and why it's better. Short on purpose. Structured the way Steve Jobs lays out a keynote: name the category, set up the problem, show why every alternative falls short, then reveal why ours wins.

Grounded in [competitor-reviews-and-positioning](competitor-reviews-and-positioning.md) (what real users love and hate about the tools we compete with).

---

## The category

The full outbound sales stack: **dialer + email sequencer + CRM + call/meeting intelligence + data enrichment** — in one product, for the SMB and solo seller.

**Why here.** It's a huge market, and *nobody has done it well*. Every incumbent is either enterprise-only, single-purpose, or predatory on price. There is no market leader for the 2–30 rep team. That's the opening.

---

## Set up the problem

Picture the rep's actual day. They finish a call. Now the flow breaks:

- Find the next person. Look at their website. Figure out the angle.
- Update the CRM on the call that just ended.
- Write and send the follow-up.
- Then dial again.

**A hundred little clicks between every conversation.** It's slow, it kills momentum, and it's the part reps skip when they're tired. And it's nearly impossible to get every rep to do it well and the same way — which is why sales managers spend their whole week role-playing, practicing, and monitoring.

Now look at what a rep has to buy to cover all that, and how each option fails:

| To get… | They buy… | And the complaint is… |
|---|---|---|
| Call intelligence | Gong | Enterprise-only, ~$250/seat + $5k platform fee, "breaks under 30 reps" |
| A CRM | HubSpot / Attio | HubSpot pricing escalates + forced annual contracts; Attio has weak reporting |
| A dialer | Nooks / Orum / Salesloft | ~$500–5k/seat, connect-delay "robocall" lag, or no native dialer at all |
| A fast email client | Superhuman | No team workflows, subscription per seat |
| Enrichment | Clay / Apollo | Clay needs a specialist + credit-burn; Apollo data ~19% valid |

So the rep ends up paying for **four or five tools, gluing them together, and tab-switching between them all day** — and still doing the hundred clicks by hand. That's the villain.

## The tagline

> **The fastest CRM experience ever built — like Superhuman, for the whole sales motion.**

The ideal workflow: you talk, and you hit Enter. A thousand times a day. The next person is called, the follow-up is sent, the CRM is updated — all on one keystroke, without leaving the screen.

## Why ours is better — the three principles

### 1. Pricing is the wedge

Everyone else charges expensive per-seat subscriptions with annual lock-in. **We are entirely usage-based, with a free tier that gives you everything.**

Experience the whole product at the earliest stage — one user or a large team — and scale to unlimited usage. Apollo and Clay are the closest to this model, and *even they* still have subscriptions. We don't.

- **Contrast:** Gong-grade call intelligence + a modern CRM + a fast email client, priced for a 5-person team. No annual contract, no platform fee, live the same day.
- **Downside to hold:** usage-based means we must make cost visible and predictable, or it becomes the next Clay credit-anxiety complaint.

### 2. All-in-one, and better than each tool it replaces

Other CRMs take an *ecosystem* approach: little core innovation, big feature gaps, and a pile of integrations to third-party tools. **We do the opposite — we build everything you need, and a better version than each standalone tool.**

- **Downside:** some people genuinely like their existing tools.
- **Why we win anyway:**
  1. **Easy way into any account.** We can run a bake-off against whichever single tool they already have.
  2. **AI makes it possible now.** A small — even one-person — team can reach feature-completeness with all of these tools far faster than before.
  3. **One surface shows everything.** The right experience is *one screen* for all of it, with multiple features working at the same time. No tab-switching, no glue.
  4. **Cheaper for the customer.** One tool, not five. You don't pay for as much.

### 3. Keep the rep in flow — talk, hit Enter, repeat

The belief underneath everything: the biggest problem in sales tools today is the **time-consuming, flow-breaking work between conversations**, and how hard it is to get every rep to do the right next thing consistently.

With AI and good evals, we can:

- Compute the **next best action** and put it one keystroke away.
- **Keep the rep in flow** — no hunting, no manual CRM updates, no context-switching.
- Give reps more **stamina** across a long day of dials.
- Get to the **right action quicker**, the same way every time — without a manager role-playing it into them.

SMB and solo outbound teams (~2–30 reps): founder-led sellers, agencies, recruiters, real estate, insurance, and other phone-heavy verticals that live in the dialer and can't afford a Gong + Clay + HubSpot stack. The exact segment every incumbent underserves.
